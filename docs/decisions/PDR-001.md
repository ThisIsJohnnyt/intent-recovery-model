# PDR-001: Build dataset infrastructure before large datasets

**Date**: 2026-07-28
**Status**: Accepted

## Decision

We will build the dataset infrastructure — specification, review process,
and benchmark suite — before we build large datasets.

## Reasoning

A well-defined specification, review process, and benchmark suite are more
valuable than generating thousands of examples without quality controls.
Every future dataset should be reproducible, reviewable, and versioned.

Concretely, this means: `training/DATASET_SPEC.md` (the generation rules
and schema), `docs/datasets/REVIEW_GUIDE.md` (the rubric a batch must pass),
and `docs/benchmarks/benchmark_suite.md` (how quality gets measured) exist
and are followed *before* scaling synthetic generation up from a handful of
examples to hundreds or thousands.

This is also why the gold tier (`datasets/gold/`) exists at all: a small,
hand-curated, fully-explainable batch to validate the schema and process at
low cost, rather than discovering process problems after generating a large
volume of examples.
